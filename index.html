Business Plan Questionnaire

A guided starting point for describing your business idea, customers, market, operations and next steps.

How this works: Answer in your own words. If you do not know something yet, write “Not sure yet.” Your answers can be downloaded as a Markdown draft for review and development. This questionnaire does not calculate revenue, costs, profitability, cash flow or loan repayment capacity.

4. Customers, market and competitors

Describe how the business will work day to day.

7. Risks, funding purpose and next steps

Identify challenges and how you will respond. Do not enter financial projections here.
