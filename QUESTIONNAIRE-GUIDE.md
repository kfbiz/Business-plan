# KF Biz Business Plan Questionnaire — Question Guide

This questionnaire uses the topics in the user's supplied Job Market Finland business-plan document as its main reference. It is an independent KF Biz planning aid, not the official government service.

## How to answer
- Answer each question directly and specifically.
- Distinguish facts from assumptions.
- Where possible, include evidence such as customer conversations, market research, competitor examples, qualifications or supplier discussions.
- If you do not know an answer yet, write "To be confirmed" and list how you will check it.
- The questionnaire supports business-plan writing. It does not decide Starttiraha eligibility or guarantee financing.

## 1. Business idea and starting points
1. What is this business plan for: Starttiraha preparation, establishing a new company, financing preparation, or developing an existing business?
2. What stage is the business at?
3. What is the proposed or registered company name? Why did you choose it?
4. What legal form are you considering, and why might it suit the business?
5. Where will the business operate, and in which industry?
6. When do you plan to start?
7. Describe the business idea simply: what will you do, for whom, and how?
8. Why do you want to start or develop this business? Where did the idea come from?
9. What are your goals, and how do you imagine the business developing over time?

## 2. Entrepreneur's skills and readiness
1. Who will establish and run the business? Describe relevant experience, education, qualifications and skills.
2. Which skills do you or your team already have in practical entrepreneurship, industry knowledge, applicable rules, financial administration, marketing, sales and management?
3. What preparation have you completed so far?
4. What skills or knowledge are missing, and how will you obtain them?
5. How will you organise invoicing, bookkeeping, expense records and financial administration?
6. If there are co-founders or key people, how will responsibilities be divided?

## 3. Products, services and customer value
Repeat the following questions for each main product, service or product/service group:
1. What is its name?
2. What does it include, and what exactly does the customer receive?
3. What pricing approach will you use?
4. Which customer group is it for, and what need does it address?
5. Which advantages may make it attractive compared with alternatives? Consider price, quality, value for money, availability, delivery speed, location, uniqueness, customer experience, brand awareness, responsibility, environmental impact and terms.
6. What evidence supports your view that it is competitive?
7. Will you need to consider trademarks, design rights, patents or other intellectual-property protection?

## 4. Customers, market and competitors
1. Who are your target customers? Describe their location, needs and buying behaviour.
2. What evidence suggests there is demand for your offer?
3. What geographical area or online market will you serve?
4. What is happening in your industry, and what is the competitive situation?
5. Who are the main competitors or alternatives, and what do they offer?
6. How will your business stand out?
7. What partners, suppliers and professional networks may be important?
8. What market assumptions or questions still need to be checked?

## 5. Marketing and customer acquisition
1. Which marketing channels will you use? Consider social media, website, paid advertising, email, community platforms, influencers, traditional media, signs, printed materials, events, networks and referrals.
2. Why are those channels suitable for your target customers?
3. What brand image do you want to build?
4. How will you review whether marketing is working?
5. Who will be responsible for marketing and content creation?

## 6. Sales and customer relationships
1. Where or how will customers buy from you? Consider your website or online store, third-party platforms, social media, business premises, retailers, partners, events, pop-ups and direct contact.
2. Describe the process from first contact to purchase and delivery.
3. Who will handle sales and customer service?
4. How will you build trust, encourage repeat business and earn referrals?

## 7. Operations, rules and responsibility
1. What are the main steps in delivering your product or service?
2. What premises, equipment, software, materials or suppliers will you need?
3. What permits, notifications, registrations, qualifications or industry rules may apply in Finland?
4. What insurance or other protection should you investigate?
5. How will you act responsibly towards customers, employees, society and the environment?

## 8. Risks and SWOT analysis
1. Which risks may affect your business: economic conditions, rising supplier costs, weak demand, industry or technology changes, legislation, competition, supplier/partner issues, changing customer needs, recruitment, sales, customer acquisition or work-life balance?
2. Which risks are most important, and how might they affect the business?
3. How will you reduce the risks or respond if they occur?
4. **Strengths:** What internal skills, experience, relationships or advantages do you have?
5. **Weaknesses:** What internal gaps or limitations need attention?
6. **Opportunities:** What external trends, customer groups, technologies or partnerships could help?
7. **Threats:** What external factors could harm the business?

## 9. Entrepreneur's everyday routine and wellbeing
1. How will you plan for your personal livelihood while developing the business?
2. What will a typical working week look like, including customer work, administration, marketing and sales?
3. How will you plan holidays, days off and unexpected absences?
4. How will you maintain your health, wellbeing and ability to continue working?

## 10. If you plan to employ people
Complete this section only if relevant.
1. What staff will you need, for which roles and at what stage?
2. How will you recruit suitable people?
3. What experience or support prepares you to supervise employees?
4. How will you orient new employees and explain their duties and safety practices?
5. How will you support staff wellbeing, skills development and job satisfaction?

## 11. Future development, vision and values
1. What trends or changes could affect your industry in the next few years?
2. How might your customers and their needs change?
3. How will you keep your products or services relevant and improve them?
4. What would you like the company to look like in about five years, and how might you get there?
5. What values matter to the company, and how will they guide everyday decisions?

## 12. Next steps and application-specific questions
1. What actions must be completed before launch and during the first stage?
2. What questions, advice or support do you still need?
3. If seeking funding, what business purpose would the funding serve?

### Additional prompts for Starttiraha preparation
- Do you plan to work full-time in the business? Explain the intended arrangement.
- Have you already started full-time business activity, or are you still preparing? Record dates accurately and check current official application timing instructions before acting.
- How do you plan to support yourself during the initial stage? Describe the approach in words; prepare calculations separately if required.

### Additional prompts for Finnvera or another lender
- What business need or development goal makes external financing relevant?
- What alternatives have you considered, and why might financing be appropriate?
- What operational factors—such as customer relationships, experience, contracts, delivery capability and risk controls—could support the business's ability to meet obligations?

## Scope boundary: financial calculations
This questionnaire is for business planning and written analysis. It does **not** calculate revenue forecasts, costs, profitability, cash flow, break-even or loan repayment capacity. Prepare financial forecasts and supporting documents separately if required by the authority or lender.

## Final review checklist
- Verify market claims and add sources or evidence.
- Confirm relevant permits, registrations, insurance and industry-specific obligations.
- Check the latest requirements of the relevant authority or lender.
- Prepare separate financial forecasts and supporting documents if required.
- Ensure the plan is realistic, accurate and consistent before submission.

Starttiraha and financing decisions are made by the relevant authority or lender. This guide does not guarantee eligibility or approval.
